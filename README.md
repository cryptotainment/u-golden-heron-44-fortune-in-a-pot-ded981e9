A make.fun entry for **Fortune in a Pot** — one kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, at most 120 characters.

`main.py` writes a single deterministic line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A sunny week is coming: your leaves will glow, your keeper will brag, and the cactus will pretend not to mind.

Run it with `python3 main.py`.
